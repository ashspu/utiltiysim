What is reconstructed

The full approved image is vector artwork: roads, property linework, gardens, fields, hedgerows, woodland edges, water, tracks and farm buildings. No raster image is embedded in the SVG.

205 Orchard, Garden, Hazel, Market North, Riverside West, Meadow West, Meadow East, Parkside North and Willow Bend properties have individually authored parcels, footprints and driveways, linked to existing UtilitySim IDs. Their 3D study uses those same polygons, recorded gable/hip roof types, wall heights and story counts. Roof pitch and window placement are illustrative. Solar modules sit on individual roof faces. Two rear Riverside lots use explicitly authored shared driveways, whose layout is a draft interpretation. Two traced outbuildings belong to their parent properties and have no separate customer records. The remaining painted properties are visual references, not converted records. The landscape study includes 31 traced farm buildings and selectable field, woodland, greenspace and water surfaces. Farm heights are illustrative and have no assigned utility IDs. Landscape boundaries are extracted draft geometry; green areas do not imply public park ownership.

These are authoring drafts. The saved 500-home simulation still uses its previous geometry; no addresses, residents, meters or history have been moved.
